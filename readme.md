# Telecom Customer Churn & Revenue Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses customer churn, customer characteristics, service usage, and revenue performance for a telecom company using Microsoft Power BI.

The purpose of the project is to identify major churn drivers, understand the revenue impact of customer churn, identify customer segments requiring attention, and provide actionable business recommendations.

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyse the overall customer base.
- Measure customer churn and retention.
- Identify major churn drivers.
- Analyse customer demographics and tenure.
- Examine churn across contract and service types.
- Analyse revenue performance.
- Measure revenue lost through customer churn.
- Identify customer groups requiring attention.
- Provide executive recommendations and strategic actions.

## Key Findings

The analysis identified:

- A total of 7,043 customers.
- 1,869 churned customers.
- An overall churn rate of 26.54%.
- A retention rate of 73.46%.
- An average customer tenure of 32.37 months.
- An average monthly charge of 64.76.
- Month-to-month customers as an important churn-risk group.
- Fiber optic customers showing a relatively high churn rate.
- Higher churn among customers without online security and technical support.
- Approximately 140K in revenue lost through churn by contract in the displayed analysis.

## Customer Segmentation

The dataset does not contain a predefined Customer Segment field. Therefore, tenure groups were used as a practical customer segmentation approach for the revenue analysis.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were developed:

1. Develop targeted retention campaigns for high-risk customers.
2. Encourage suitable month-to-month customers to move to longer-term contracts.
3. Investigate the high churn rate among fiber optic customers.
4. Promote online security and technical support services.
5. Strengthen engagement with customers during their first 12 months.
6. Continue monitoring churn using Power BI to identify emerging customer risks.

## Strategic Actions

The recommended strategic actions include:

| Strategic Action | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|
| Target month-to-month customers | Reduce customer churn |
| Improve early-tenure engagement | Increase first-year retention |
| Investigate fiber optic churn | Improve customer experience |
| Promote security and technical support | Increase customer value |
| Encourage longer-term contracts | Improve customer retention |
| Monitor churn regularly | Enable early intervention |
